Privacy Policy

Hypothetic Studio · Effective November 26, 2026

1. Whose data

Hypothetic Studio is used only by the owner of the Hypothetic World accounts. It does not collect data from any other person.

2. What is used

  • OAuth access tokens and basic profile information (account ID, display name) for the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.
  • The owner's own video files and captions that are being published.

3. Where it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in the operating system's credential store. Nothing is sent to any server other than the official YouTube, TikTok and Instagram APIs.

4. Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with any third party.

5. Deletion

The owner can disconnect an account at any time inside the tool, which deletes the stored token, and can also revoke access in each platform's account settings.
